# Safar Link

> **Keep every journey moving.**

Safar Link is a transport operations system built for bus companies, terminals and staff who need one clear place to manage daily movement.

It brings the practical workflows around passenger travel and parcel movement together so staff can spend less time chasing information and more time moving people and items.

## What Safar Link does

### Parcel tracking

Safar Link gives parcels a clear record from the moment they are registered.

Staff can:

- Register sender and recipient details
- Record the item category, quantity and description
- Choose a destination terminal
- Assign a bus schedule when needed
- Generate and use a unique tracking code
- Search parcels by tracking code, sender or recipient
- Follow movement through clear statuses
- Check the latest update and destination
- See when an item has arrived and is ready for collection

The goal is simple: when someone asks, “Where is this parcel?”, the answer should be visible without a chain of phone calls.

## Passenger tickets

The ticket workflow keeps passenger bookings connected to the trip they belong to.

Staff can record:

- Passenger name and phone number
- Origin and destination
- Bus schedule
- Seat number
- Fare
- Booking status

This gives the team a cleaner picture of who is travelling and which trip their booking belongs to.

## Bus schedules

Safar Link provides one shared place for bus departure information.

Staff can manage:

- Bus company
- Bus number
- Origin
- Destination
- Departure time
- Arrival time
- Operating days

Schedules can then be used by the parcel and ticket workflows instead of being kept as a separate list.

## Terminal operations overview

The overview screen is designed for the start of a shift. It gives staff a quick read on the operation, including:

- Parcels currently in transit
- Parcels that arrived today
- Tickets booked for the day
- The next departure
- Incoming parcels
- Upcoming bus departures

It is built to answer the questions staff usually need answered first: **what is moving, what has arrived, and what leaves next?**

## Staff workflow

Safar Link is intended for operational teams rather than public self-service signup.

Staff accounts are created by the organisation's Safar Link administrator. Once signed in, each staff member can work with the workflows available to their operation.

## Parcel statuses

Parcel movement can be represented with clear operational stages:

**Registered → Loaded → In transit → Arrived → Collected**

The status trail gives staff and receiving terminals a simple way to understand where an item is in its journey.

## Search and daily usability

Safar Link is designed for quick terminal work. Common actions such as finding a parcel, locating a passenger or checking the next departure are kept close to the main workflow.

The interface uses clear labels, strong visual hierarchy and large primary actions so a busy member of staff does not have to learn a complicated system before they can use it.

## Who it is for

Safar Link is suited to transport organisations that handle recurring passenger and parcel movement between terminals, including:

- Bus companies
- Passenger transport operators
- Terminal teams
- Parcel and luggage desks
- Dispatch and route staff
- Organisations operating multiple destinations

**Safar Link connects the everyday movement of buses, passengers and parcels into one operational workspace.**
